# MiniSense Operations & Policy Knowledge Base

## Q1: What is the refund and cancellation policy for subscription plans?
All self-serve subscriptions (Free, Starter, and Standard tiers) are billed in advance on monthly or annual cycles. We do not provide prorated refunds or mid-cycle partial credits when an account downgrades or cancels early; platform access and current plan privileges remain fully operational until the end of the paid billing period. Enterprise agreements with annual commitments may request tailored commercial terms and termination credit clauses subject to Master Services Agreement (MSA) review.

## Q2: How are pricing tiers, seat allowances, and export quotas structured?
Our Free plan allows up to 100 survey response exports per month in CSV format with 1 administrative user seat. The Starter plan provides up to 5 team seats, unlimited CSV and Excel spreadsheet exports, and weekly automated summary reports. The Enterprise plan removes all data limits, enabling unlimited CSV, JSON, and PDF report downloads, multi-team permission roles, dedicated Snowflake and BigQuery data warehouse connectors, and production REST API access.

## Q3: What operational standards and express pickup guidelines govern wait times?
Kitchen and front-counter operations target a maximum wait time of 5 to 7 minutes for non-peak orders and under 12 minutes during peak rush periods. Beginning May 1st, express mobile pickup lanes were introduced at all flagship locations to expedite digital orders. If wait times exceed 20 minutes due to unexpected operational bottlenecks, store supervisors are authorized to issue immediate complimentary beverage vouchers or loyalty credits.

## Q4: What features, offline capabilities, and OS requirements are supported in App v2.0?
The redesigned MiniSense Mobile App (v2.0, released late April) features one-tap digital payments, real-time push tracking from kitchen to counter, contactless table ordering, and localized loyalty rewards. It includes offline caching for order receipts and supports iOS 15+ and Android 11+. Legacy versions below v2.0 experienced Bluetooth check-in and checkout stability issues and should be updated immediately via the App Store or Google Play.

## Q5: How do customer loyalty tiers, points accumulation, and reward expirations work?
Customers earn 10 reward points per dollar spent across all channels. Members reach VIP status upon accumulating 2,500 points within a calendar year, unlocking free priority shipping, monthly exclusive tasting events, and dedicated phone support. Reward points remain valid for 12 months of account activity; an automated email alert is sent 30 days prior to any pending point expiration. Points cannot be redeemed for cash or transferred between accounts.

## Q6: What support response SLAs are guaranteed by account tier?
Community forum access and automated knowledge documentation are available to all users 24/7. Starter tier subscribers receive ticketing and email support with a guaranteed response within 24 business hours. Enterprise clients are assigned a named Technical Account Manager (TAM) and benefit from a 1-hour critical incident response SLA with round-the-clock 24/7 phone and Slack escalation channels.

## Q7: What cleanliness, hygiene, and food preparation protocols are enforced?
Every retail and dining location follows strict sanitation guidelines, including mandatory hand-washing cycles every 30 minutes, hourly sanitization of high-touch surfaces, and continuous digital temperature logging for all refrigeration units. High-traffic facility areas such as dining lounges and restrooms undergo documented physical inspections every two hours during service hours.

## Q8: What accessibility features and facility amenities are provided at store locations?
All retail locations comply fully with ADA accessibility standards, providing wheelchair-accessible entrances, lowered transaction counters, and dedicated accessible parking bays. High-speed guest Wi-Fi is provided complimentary for all visitors, alongside quiet workspace seating zones equipped with USB-C and standard charging outlets.
