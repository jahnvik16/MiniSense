# GreenLeaf Bistro — Customer Experience FAQ

## Q1: What are your most popular menu items?
Our top sellers are the Avocado Toast, Garden Bowl, and Cold Brew Coffee. All menu items emphasize fresh, locally sourced organic produce, whole grains, and artisanal dressings prepared from scratch every morning. Seasonal specials rotate quarterly based on regional harvest availability. For guests with specialized dietary requirements, gluten-free toast substitutions, dairy-free milk alternatives (oat and almond), and plant-based protein add-ons such as marinated tofu and roasted chickpeas are readily available across all order channels.

## Q2: What is your average wait time?
We target under 10 minutes for counter orders during off-peak hours. Peak hours (12:00 PM–1:00 PM lunch and 6:00 PM–8:00 PM dinner) may see 15–20 minute waits due to high kitchen order volume. Beginning May 1st, dedicated express pickup stations were introduced across all bistro locations to separate advance mobile orders from walk-in registers. Guests ordering through our mobile app receive real-time kitchen tracking notifications with estimated preparation windows to help minimize in-store waiting.

## Q3: How do you handle complaints?
All complaints are escalated to the shift manager within 15 minutes. Refunds or replacements are offered for quality issues. If an order takes longer than 20 minutes due to unexpected kitchen bottlenecks, supervisors are empowered to issue immediate complimentary beverage vouchers or GreenLeaf loyalty points. We conduct daily shift debriefs to review all guest feedback submitted via digital receipts or counter cards, ensuring operational issues are resolved swiftly.

## Q4: What is your CSAT target?
We aim for an CSAT of 4.5+. Scores below 4.0 trigger a root-cause review with the operations team. Store general managers evaluate weekly customer satisfaction metrics across individual categories—including food temperature, cleanliness, order accuracy, and cashier friendliness. Corrective action plans, which may involve additional station prep training or adjusted shift staffing allocations during rush hours, must be submitted within 48 hours of any score dip.

## Q5: How do group orders, catering, and table reservations work?
We operate primarily as a fast-casual dining experience with open seating on a first-come, first-served basis, though patio reservations are available for evening dining parties of six or more. Large group catering orders must be placed at least 24 hours in advance via our online catering desk. Catering packages feature individually boxed lunches, sustainable compostable cutlery, and guaranteed delivery within a scheduled 15-minute delivery window.

## Q6: What cleanliness, hygiene, and kitchen sanitation protocols are enforced?
Every GreenLeaf Bistro location follows comprehensive food safety standards. Kitchen prep stations, cutting surfaces, and cashier touchpoints are sanitized every 30 minutes, with team members adhering to mandatory hourly handwashing timers. Refrigeration units and cold-prep lines maintain strict digital temperature logs between 34°F and 38°F. Dining room tables, beverage condiment stands, and restrooms undergo documented physical inspections every hour during business operations.

## Q7: How does the GreenLeaf Rewards loyalty program work?
Guests earn 10 GreenLeaf points per dollar spent on counter purchases, kiosk orders, and mobile pickups. Accumulating 500 points unlocks a free artisanal beverage, soup, or side salad, while 1,000 points unlocks any signature entree bowl or toast. Reward points remain active for 12 months from the date of issuance. Members receive double-point promotions on seasonal menu launches, complimentary birthday treats, and contactless mobile pay directly within the app.
